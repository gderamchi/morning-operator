A quieter morning,
drawn in pixels.

Fourteen directions were tried for MORE, away from glass and cards. One is recommended: Horizon. Two runner-ups follow, then every exploration with a one-line verdict. The current app is untouched; this is a side branch.

Recommended

Horizon

One line divides sky from ground. What is still to come lives in the sky; what is done has sunk into the grass. As the morning advances the line moves down and a pixel sun climbs over it. No cards, no glass, no icons: paper, grass, one orange, two typefaces, and pixels only where the two meet. Before dawn the sky is night and the horizon glows through a dithered band that widens as the morning goes.

It borrows the mood of lowcortisol.dev (deep grass, bone, clay, grain, a soft serif italic beside a light mono) and gives it one meaningful mechanic. The pixels never carry meaning alone: the heading, the two lists and “4 of 7 · 3:40” say everything the picture says.

The other screens

The one motion

When a moment completes, the horizon moves down by that moment’s share of the planned minutes and the sun climbs one pixel row. 600 ms, ease-in-out. Nothing else animates: no springs, no parallax, no glow.

Before dawn the dithered band above the horizon widens with the same step. With Reduce Motion the heights snap and the moving row crossfades. The loop on the left is a CSS demonstration; the SwiftUI proof on this branch (--design-preview, DEBUG only) does the same with Canvas.

Full tokens, type, pixel rules and accessibility rules: SPEC.md.

Runner-ups

Risograph

Two inks on cream, a halftone sun, slightly misregistered. The most editorial and the most confident; it would photograph beautifully. It is louder than a 6 am screen wants to be, and the halftone needs careful containment near text. Keep it for marketing, or as a seasonal paper.

One bit

Black on paper, a bitmap face, real checkboxes. The most original and oddly the most legible; it is also the easiest to build. It risks reading as a toy, and dark mode is simply the inverse, which is fine but offers no dawn. The best alternative if Horizon feels too soft.
