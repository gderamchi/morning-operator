## One home, details when needed

Morning opens with a smaller system date, lowercase **morning** title and simple total minutes. Repeated Planned/Stopped labels and filler captions are removed. Compact tappable tiles show relative **+Nm** offsets based on preceding planned durations; completed/skipped moments retain those explicit states. These offsets are neither appointments nor elapsed-time measurements. Tapping opens the real detail, including an honest purpose and the saved or available sourced content. There is no large primary button, permanent weekday strip or permanent tab bar. The date remains a route to the selected period in Review.

A small sunrise control at the bottom-right anchors a native system popover with labeled Start/playback, Update, Past mornings, Settings and Learn actions. Tapping outside or choosing Close dismisses it. Start obeys approval rules; a draft still requires review. Its icon retains a 48-point hit target. First use keeps Learn discoverable. Review, settings and saved thoughts remain reachable, with a clear return to Morning.

Selecting a destination first dismisses the popover while retaining presentation ownership. The queued action executes only in the same foreground data generation when ordinary work remains allowed; cancellation, backgrounding or local forgetting drops it. This keeps an invitation/Shortcut from starting a competing transition in the dismissal gap. Scoped native journeys verify outside dismissal, destination changes and deferred entry; broader interruption coverage remains open.

The **current** running moment's detail provides Pause, Next and Again; a paused moment provides Continue and Again. Next advances explicitly. Again invokes the existing supported read path and preserves its source checks; it does not claim to replay an arbitrary external activity. Other moments remain readable without gaining controls for the active one. Reordering still lives in idle detail controls and native drag.

Tiles support native drag reordering on iOS 27 and accessible Move earlier/Move later alternatives in the detail sheet. A completed move saves an unapproved draft through the existing persistence boundary; a canceled, stale or unchanged move makes no edit. Active runs and busy mutations disable reordering. There is no implicit approval or change to an executing session.

The default portrait target is **up to ten moments without scrolling** on the target phone. Larger Dynamic Type, smaller heights and landscape use an accessible scrolling/detail fallback instead of clipping text, hiding Stop or shrinking controls below usable size. Extra moments stay reachable. At `de6d4b1`, ten-moment idle/active portrait fit, native drag persistence, the earlier expanded menu and landscape reachability passed simulator checks; largest accessibility text passed separately. The newer compact popover/detail changes have fourteen clean native journey passes plus final state/accessibility regressions recorded in PHASE-ONE. An owner walkthrough and VoiceOver/reduced-motion checks remain open.

Active recording status and Stop remain available; completed observations and corrections live in Review. Apple's consent/picker remains system UI. A wake-time entry point never substitutes for a new required recording choice.

Review starts with the selected period rather than a large editorial heading. When dated observations exist, their compact, partitioned recurrence overview comes first and routine-run timing is secondary. Its day pages show separate clip previews with unknown moments and links to original samples. These are source glimpses, not a synthesized typical routine or measured app-use timeline.

Review now keeps saved observations across imports. Its day pages distinguish observations from routine runs; **Saved observations** reaches older entries and **Date needed** handles missing observed dates. Original extraction and corrected notes are separate, with explicit day/zone/origin assignment and scoped deletion. The compact Morning gains no archive controls or counters. Routine preferences also stay in Review: editing a moment can keep it in later suggestions or remember its planned minutes; removing a row distinguishes this draft from future exclusion. Saved choices can be cleared without changing the current morning. These are explicit choices, not inferred habits. **From your observations** opens the selected period's sampled labels with separate zone/origin groups and labeled/unclassified/missing-day counts. Each label leads to exact source quotations; each clip exposes ordered sampled moments, minimum visible returns and unresolved points. Corrections invalidate old interpretations while preserving originals. These are distinct-date appearances, not comparable full mornings or observed-habit averages. See [Observed mornings](OBSERVED-MORNINGS.md).

## Native presentation

The design rationale is a readable plan with a small, distinct control layer. Apple reserves Liquid Glass primarily for navigation and controls; clickable tiles are controls, while long notes and evidence belong on readable detail surfaces. Use restrained tint and avoid nested glass panels or a glass background behind every paragraph. Apple's current Design Award examples offer useful references: **Moonlitt**, the 2026 Interaction winner, for simple interactions and native glass; **Tide Guide**, the Visuals and Graphics winner, for clear information hierarchy; **Guitar Wiz**, an Inclusivity winner, for accessibility. These are references, not templates to copy. [Apple materials guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials), [2026 Apple Design Awards](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/).

Native `.glassEffect`, `GlassEffectContainer`, `.glassEffectID` and glass button styles are available on **iOS 26+**. Apply the effect to the final control bounds and use system interaction/transition behavior. A container can coordinate the sunrise control and its expanded actions; spacing must not accidentally merge separate tiles. Prefer regular glass for legible controls. [Custom Liquid Glass](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/applying-liquid-glass-to-custom-views), [glassEffect(_:in:)](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/view/glasseffect(_:in:)), [GlassEffectContainer](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/glasseffectcontainer).

The app uses SwiftUI [`popover(isPresented:attachmentAnchor:arrowEdge:content:)`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/view/popover%28ispresented%3Aattachmentanchor%3Aarrowedge%3Acontent%3A%29) anchored to the sunrise, with [`presentationCompactAdaptation(_:)`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/view/presentationcompactadaptation%28_%3A%29) requesting a popover in compact layouts. This is an adaptation preference; SwiftUI owns placement, material and presentation motion. The app does not reproduce or promise a specific Apple Music animation. Scrollable action content and an explicit Close control preserve reachability at larger text sizes.

SwiftUI's native `ForEach.reorderable()` and `reorderContainer(for:isEnabled:move:)` are **iOS 27+**. Their completion difference identifies moved items and the destination; application code must validate stable IDs and commit once. The iOS 26 baseline needs an availability-gated fallback, such as native List moves or an explicit supported drag/drop implementation, plus accessible move actions. Do not raise the app's minimum OS implicitly. [Apple's native reordering example](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/271/).

Respect Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency, meaningful VoiceOver labels, readable contrast and accessible period navigation. Reduced-transparency content should use an opaque treatment. [Reduce Motion](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/environmentvalues/accessibilityreducemotion), [Reduce Transparency](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/environmentvalues/accessibilityreducetransparency).

## Start entry: existing behavior and separate spikes

The **one-time morning invitation** and **Start morning App Shortcut** already exist. Settings exposes the invitation's explicit date/time, permission, cancellation and recovery controls. Morning also has a direct clock control on Morning to open the same Set/Edit invitation sheet and display an upcoming time. It is not a Clock alarm. A notification tap or explicit foreground Shortcut request enters the guarded start path: approved routines can start/resume, drafts require review, and a busy or inactive app waits. Delivery alone does not start speech, a routine or recording. Native checks confirm that opening the time editor schedules nothing and that a pending start waits for dismissal. This grants no new notification or capture permission. [Current entry implementation and limits](MORNING-ENTRY.md).

A person can configure Shortcuts **Alarm**, **Sleep/Waking Up** or **Time of Day** triggers; testing those with this app remains a separate spike. Apple's current guide also lists a Notification trigger, which is a configured automation capability, not general notification-inbox permission for this app. Foreground/authentication and system conditions still apply to the existing Start action. No global physical-pickup trigger or silent screen-recording start is promised. DeviceActivity's first use within a monitored interval is a different signal and does not replace Apple's capture choice. [Apple event triggers](https://support.apple.com/guide/shortcuts/event-triggers-apd932ff833f/ios), [DeviceActivityCenter](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivitycenter).

## What a number means

- **Planned minutes** belong to the approved routine or an unapproved proposal; they are estimates.
- **Recorded routine time** comes from Morning Operator's session events, excludes pauses where stated and becomes unknown after interrupted timing. It does not measure other apps or time saved.
- **Observed app activity** requires connected, timestamped evidence and its own coverage. Configured Shortcuts callbacks are partial entries/returns, not pickups, dwell time, reels or reading intent.
- **Typical observed morning** requires comparable evidence days and per-metric denominators. Until that collector/profile exists, show the plan or real run history with those names. Missing values stay unknown; samples remain outside personal averages.

The compact design does not complete multi-day learning. [#21](https://github.com/gibouu/morning-operator/issues/21) owns valid metrics and [#11](https://github.com/gibouu/morning-operator/issues/11) owns adaptive profiles.

## Completion and voice

There is no Notes destination. A saved completed morning offers **Speak**, **Write** and **Not now**. With read-aloud enabled, a brief spoken invitation can accompany that offer; it never starts the microphone. Speak explicitly opens one English or French dictation turn, while Write opens the existing editor. Not now dismisses the invitation without changing the completed run.

Dictation requires on-device recognition and allows up to **30 seconds of input plus three seconds for a final result**, bounded to 4,000 characters. Playback must release audio ownership before listening, and microphone ownership must release before the words enter review. The final text opens an editable thought/dream/reminder/calendar draft; nothing is saved or sent to an Apple app until the person explicitly saves that reviewed draft. Saved voice-origin captures show **Spoken with MORE** under **Review → Saved thoughts**. The app keeps no audio file; the unsaved transcript and review remain in memory.

Cancel, backgrounding or a changed completed session invalidates the listening attempt and its late callbacks. A canceled turn cannot later open review or save a thought. Permission failures retain the typed route, and unresolved microphone cleanup blocks new audio until released. This is one explicit dictation turn, separate from routine commands and foreground Siri capture; it does not provide an always-listening keyword or an open conversation. The three synthetic completion-flow checks pass, as does the existing command-entry check. Physical microphone, recognition and audio-route behavior remain unverified. The editor keyboard transition emits an unresolved layout warning; see [verification](PHASE-ONE.md).

Voice settings remain reachable through Settings. The existing stored choice is an optional `voiceIdentifier`: **Automatic** when empty, or a selected installed Apple voice. Current source ranks Automatic by device locale/dialect, language and reported quality; unavailable selections fall back to Automatic. The chooser excludes Personal/Novelty and non-Apple providers. Preview/Stop and a genuinely paused Resume state are distinct. There is no stored rate, pitch or custom language preference; speech uses Apple's default rate. The current read-aloud and hands-free preferences remain separate from voice selection.

An explicit [push-to-listen command turn](VOICE.md) has native source and earlier simulator checks. The parser accepts the exact English phrases **again** and **read again** as repeat aliases, with the same current-step and one-turn guards; it adds no listening trigger or general conversational intent. All ten focused command tests pass; real microphone recognition remains unverified. Custom voice enrollment, guaranteed Siri voice, a full conversational recap and audible HomePod output remain future work. The owner has not confirmed hearing the earlier phone checks. Unsupported routine operations still wait for a real user/adaptor outcome; speech finishing does not prove an external task was performed. [#4](https://github.com/gibouu/morning-operator/issues/4), [#6](https://github.com/gibouu/morning-operator/issues/6), [#20](https://github.com/gibouu/morning-operator/issues/20).
